# Dashboard Design

## Global Finance & Sales Performance Dashboard

### Objective
Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard for business stakeholders to analyze sales and financial performance.

### Tools Used
- Power BI
- DAX
- Google Slides / Microsoft PowerPoint

### Dataset
Global Finance & Sales Performance dataset containing sales and financial information such as Revenue, COGS, Budget Target, Region, Product Category, Sales Channel, CSAT and Discount Percentage.

### Dashboard Features
- Total Revenue KPI
- Total COGS KPI
- Total Profit KPI
- Profit Margin KPI
- Revenue Trend Over Time
- Profit by Product Category
- Revenue by Sales Channel
- Region filter
- Product Category filter
- Date filter

### Key Insights
- Total Revenue: 318M
- Total COGS: 149M
- Total Profit: 169M
- Profit Margin: 53.23%
- Enterprise Software generated the highest profit among the product categories.
- Partner and Online channels generated slightly higher revenue than Direct.

### Deliverables
- Interactive Power BI Dashboard
- Dashboard Screenshot
- PPT Summary
